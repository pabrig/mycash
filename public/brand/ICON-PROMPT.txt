# Myca$h — Prompt para generador de icono (IA)

Usá la imagen `icon-original.png` como referencia de concepto (billetera = finanzas personales).
NO copies el estilo line-art ni el teal. Rediseñá con la marca Ciruela.

---

## Prompt (copiar / pegar)

```
App icon for “Myca$h”, a personal-finance PWA (Spanish-speaking users, Argentina). Purpose: track income and expenses and see how much money you have left. The icon must read instantly as a WALLET (billetera), not a folder, camera, printer, or chart.

REFERENCE INTENT (from the original icon): a wallet symbolizing money management. Keep that meaning. Do NOT keep the thin stroke line-art style, the teal accent, the upward chart/sparkline, or the near-black plate.

BRAND — Ciruela (plum / violet):
- Plate / background: #5b21b6 (deep plum)
- Primary accent: #6d28d9
- Soft lavender: #c4b5fd, #ede9fe, #a78bfa
- Wallet body / light surfaces: #faf8fd or soft white
- High contrast only within this purple family + white. No teal, green, orange, gold, or neon.

COMPOSITION (squircle app icon, 1:1):
- Solid #5b21b6 rounded-square background
- Centered horizontal bifold wallet in white / soft lavender
- Clear fold/seam across the wallet
- Right-side clasp strap with a single solid dark plum circular snap (not a camera lens / concentric rings)
- Cash bills fanned out from the top opening, with a bold fully-visible “$” on the front bill in #5b21b6
- Flat vector, minimal, modern fintech, no gradients, no shadows, no gloss, no 3D, no photo realism, no text other than “$”
- Legible at small sizes (favicon / home screen)

Style keywords: flat vector, SF Symbols clarity, fintech, minimal, high contrast, PWA app icon.
Negative: chart, sparkline, graph, folder, briefcase, camera, radio, toaster, leather texture photo, gradients, glow, skeuomorphism, busy details.
```
